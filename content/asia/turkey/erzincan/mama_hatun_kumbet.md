The Mama Hatun Kümbeti stands in Tercan, a small town about 75 kilometres east of Erzincan on the road to Erzurum. The tomb is a fine example of Seljuk funerary architecture: a cylindrical drum topped by a conical stone cap, decorated with geometric and vegetal carvings around the entrance portal. It was built around 1200 AD and is one of the best-preserved Seljuk kümbet (tomb towers) in eastern Anatolia.

The surrounding complex originally included a caravanserai — also well-preserved and worth a look — which served travellers on the Silk Road route through eastern Anatolia. The combination of tomb and caravanserai reflects the ambitions of its patron: Mama Hatun was both a regional ruler and a benefactor who understood that controlling infrastructure meant controlling trade. Tercan is an easy stop between Erzincan and Erzurum.
