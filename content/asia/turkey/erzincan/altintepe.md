Altıntepe — Turkish for "golden hill" — is an Urartian archaeological site on a small hill overlooking the Euphrates River in the Üzümlü district, about 20 kilometres east of Erzincan. The site dates from the 9th to 7th century BC and includes the remains of a fortified palace, a temple, and a necropolis with elite burials. It was discovered in 1938 during railway construction and excavated systematically in the 1960s.

The site is now partly open as an outdoor museum, with reconstruction work on some of the structural elements helping to illustrate the original layout. The hill itself offers good views across the Euphrates valley, and the geological drama of the surrounding landscape — steep ridges flanking a wide flat river plain — is evident from the top. Erzincan's archaeological museum holds finds from the site.
