Manuka is Canberra's most consistently pleasant neighbourhood for food, coffee, and relaxed urban life. The main strip along Franklin Street and Flint Street has a concentration of cafes, restaurants, delis, and wine bars that is hard to match elsewhere in the city. The suburb is popular with public servants and diplomats — the diplomatic quarter is nearby — which gives it a slightly international character.

The shopping village around Manuka Oval is compact and walkable, with fewer chains than Civic and more of the independent businesses that make for an interesting afternoon. The oval itself hosts AFL matches and cricket, with a very laid-back atmosphere compared to stadiums in larger cities. South of Manuka, the older residential streets of Griffith have some of the best early-Canberra bungalow architecture in the city.
