The Bergkerk — formally the Church of St Nicholas — is a Romanesque basilica built between 1198 and 1209 on a low hill above the IJssel riverbank. Its dedication to St Nicholas, patron saint of sailors, reflects its position overlooking the river that made Deventer's fortune: the IJssel carried Hanseatic trade into the heart of the Netherlands. The church's twin towers are among the most distinctive landmarks on the Deventer skyline.

Deconsecrated in 1967 and transferred to the municipality, the Bergkerk now serves as an exhibition hall and concert venue. The interior — spare, high-vaulted, and lit by narrow Romanesque windows — provides an excellent setting for both. The hill outside the church offers good views across the old city centre and toward the river.
