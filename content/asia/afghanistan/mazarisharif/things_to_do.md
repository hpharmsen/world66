title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Mazar-i-Sharif has two categories of attraction: the Blue Mosque and its precinct, which is the heart of the city and the reason most visitors come, and the ruins of ancient Balkh a few kilometres west. Given the security situation in Afghanistan, visits here require careful planning and up-to-date advice — but for those who make it, the Blue Mosque is one of the most extraordinary religious buildings in Central Asia.
