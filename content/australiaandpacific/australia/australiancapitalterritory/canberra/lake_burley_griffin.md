Lake Burley Griffin is an artificial lake nine kilometres long and up to 1,200 metres wide, created in 1963 by damming the Molonglo River. It lies at the exact centre of Canberra's design, with the Parliamentary Triangle to the south and the city's commercial centre to the north. The Captain Cook Memorial Jet — a water jet 147 metres high — shoots from the middle of the lake and is visible from most of the surrounding area.

The circuit around the lake (about 35 kilometres on foot or by bike) passes every major national institution in the city. Hire bikes are available from several points. The lake is used for sailing, rowing, kayaking, and dragon boating; swimming is permitted in designated areas though the water temperature limits this to summer. The Carillon — a 50-tonne bell tower on Aspen Island in the lake — gives free recitals on Wednesdays and Sundays.
