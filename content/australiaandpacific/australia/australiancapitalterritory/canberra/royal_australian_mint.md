The Royal Australian Mint in Deakin has produced all of Australia's circulating coinage since it opened in 1965. The public gallery looks down onto the factory floor, where visitors can watch coins being struck at production speed — the Mint produces around 100 million coins per week when running at full capacity. The gallery also holds a museum of Australian numismatic history and a collection of commemorative and limited-edition coins.

The Mint offers the option to strike your own one-dollar coin as a souvenir — a popular activity for younger visitors. Entry to the gallery is free, and the self-guided audio tour adds useful context about the coinage system and the Mint's history. The suburban location (10 minutes by car from the Parliamentary Triangle) means it rarely crowds, even during school holidays.
