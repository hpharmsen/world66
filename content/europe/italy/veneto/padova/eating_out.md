---
title: "Eating Out"
type: section
---

Padova is a university city, which means cheap, good food is plentiful. The area around Piazza delle Erbe and the university has many osterie serving local Veneto cuisine — bigoli (thick pasta), baccalà alla vicentina, and the excellent risotto of the region.

**Osteria L'Anfora** on Via dei Soncin is a traditional Paduan osteria with house wine by the carafe and a short, seasonal menu. **Trattoria al Pero** near the market is another reliable option for cicchetti (Venetian bar snacks) at the counter and more substantial dishes at tables. **Il Limoneto** on Via Roma has good cicchetti and local wines by the glass.

The daily market on Piazza delle Erbe and Piazza della Frutta is an excellent place to assemble a picnic. The covered market building, the Sotto il Salone, on the ground floor of the Palazzo della Ragione is full of delis, butchers, and cheese stalls.
