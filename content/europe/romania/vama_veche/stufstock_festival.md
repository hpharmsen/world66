Stufstock is Vama Veche's biggest annual event — a rock and alternative music festival held in early July that began in 2003 as an informal gathering in the reeds (the name means "stock of reeds") and grew into a full-scale festival with multiple stages, several thousand attendees, and acts ranging from Romanian indie bands to international names. It is not Glastonbury, but it captures the spirit the village was always known for: music, camping, Black Sea air, and people who prefer this to the packaged resorts further north.

Other festivals through the summer include Folk You (acoustic folk), Filmstock (outdoor cinema and short films), and the International Freedom Festival Jazz, which typically falls in August. If you are timing a visit, these events make the village come alive in a way that an ordinary summer weekend — when it is simply crowded — does not.
