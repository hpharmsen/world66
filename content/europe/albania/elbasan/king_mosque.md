The King Mosque, or Xhamia e Mbretit, is the oldest and most significant mosque inside Elbasan's castle walls. Built in the late 15th century, it is a single-domed structure in the classic early Ottoman style — modest in scale, with a small portico and a slender minaret. The interior is simple: whitewashed walls, a wooden gallery, and geometric tile work around the prayer niche.

The mosque was closed and converted during Albania's communist atheism campaign (1967–1990) but restored to religious use after the fall of communism. It is an active mosque today and open to respectful visitors outside prayer times. The location — on a quiet lane inside the walled city, a short walk from the main gate — makes it easy to combine with the rest of the old town.
