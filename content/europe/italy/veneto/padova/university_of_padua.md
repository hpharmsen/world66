The University of Padova (Università degli Studi di Padova) was founded in 1222 and is the second oldest university in Italy after Bologna. For most of its history it was the preeminent university in Europe for medicine, law, and natural philosophy, attracting students and teachers from across the continent. The main building, the Palazzo del Bo, is in the historic centre and is open for guided tours.

The highlight of the Palazzo del Bo is the Anatomical Theatre (Teatro Anatomico) from 1594 — the oldest surviving anatomical theatre in the world. It is a small, steeply raked oval room where public dissections were performed before invited audiences. The wooden tiers rise sharply around a central dissection table lit from above. It holds about 300 spectators and has the intense, concentrated atmosphere of a place where serious intellectual work was done.
