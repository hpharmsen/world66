The Speelgoedmuseum — toy museum — opened in 1932 and now holds more than 13,000 toys used in the Netherlands across several centuries. The collection is housed in two characterful medieval merchant houses in the city centre, which were built during Deventer's Hanseatic heyday. The display ranges from 18th-century carved wooden animals to tin toys, dolls, mechanical games, and postwar plastic — a comprehensive record of how Dutch children played across the centuries.

The museum is a genuine institution rather than a tourist gimmick. The older parts of the collection, particularly the 19th-century dolls' houses reproduced with extraordinary detail, are worth seeing regardless of your interest in toys specifically. Worth combining with a walk through the old centre.
