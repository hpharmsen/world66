Zeleni Vir — "Green Whirlpool" — is a gorge near the town of Skrad where a series of waterfalls descend into a crystal-clear green pool before the Kupica River flows on through the forest. The path down into the gorge takes about 20 minutes and passes through dense beech forest; wooden walkways cling to the cliff face above the falls at several points. In summer the pool is cold and clear enough to swim in, though the volume of water makes the main falls themselves dangerous to approach.

The nearby Vražji Prolaz ("Devil's Passage") is a narrow slot canyon a short walk away — in places barely wide enough for a person to pass through, the walls closing in overhead to leave only a strip of sky. Both sites together take two to three hours to explore properly. They are well signposted from the Skrad motorway exit on the A3.
