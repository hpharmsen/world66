St. Mary's is the main Orthodox church in Elbasan, located just north of the castle walls near the city centre. It was built in the 19th century and is a larger, more formally structured building than the older Church of Saint Mary inside the walls — a barrel-vaulted nave, painted interior, and a bell tower that is a modest landmark in this part of the city. The church reflects the Albanian Orthodox tradition: icons from the Berat school, traditional floor plan, and evidence of several restoration phases over the past century.

The church remained closed for most of the communist period and reopened after 1990. It now holds regular services and is accessible for visitors. Given the city's complex religious history — Orthodox churches, Ottoman mosques, and the communist destruction of both — seeing this church alongside the King Mosque inside the walls tells a compact story of Albanian history within a short walk.
