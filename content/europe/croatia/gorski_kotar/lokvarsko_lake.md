Lokvarsko Lake is a reservoir created in the 1950s by damming the Lokvarica river, now the most photogenic lake in Gorski Kotar. The forest comes down to the water's edge on three sides, and the Risnjak range fills the horizon to the west. A gravel path circles the lake (about 5 km), passing through forest and across the dam wall. The water is used for drinking water supply so swimming is not permitted, but the lake is an excellent kayaking destination — rental is available in the village of Lokve nearby.

Fishing is permitted with a licence and the lake holds trout, perch and carp. The village of Lokve, at the eastern end, has a restaurant and a small hotel. Early mornings here, when mist sits on the water and the forest is completely still, are genuinely beautiful.
