The Naziresha Mosque is a 19th-century Ottoman mosque in the lower part of Elbasan, outside the castle walls. It is smaller and more recent than the King Mosque but distinctive for its large courtyard and the decorative detail on the exterior. The building was also closed during the communist period and repurposed; it has since been restored and is an active place of worship.

It is a short walk south of the old city walls and easy to reach from the castle area. Worth a look for those interested in the full range of Elbasan's Ottoman architectural heritage — the contrast between the earlier enclosed mosque inside the walls and this more open 19th-century structure shows how Ottoman religious architecture evolved over four centuries.
