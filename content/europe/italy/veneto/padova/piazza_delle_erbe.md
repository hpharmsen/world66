The two central piazzas of Padova — Piazza delle Erbe and Piazza della Frutta — flank the Palazzo della Ragione and together form the daily heart of the city. A fruit and vegetable market has occupied these squares for centuries; it runs every morning except Sunday and is one of the most vivid in the Veneto. The produce stalls are genuine — these are working markets, not tourist displays.

The arcades around both piazzas contain shops, cafés, and bars at ground level, with residential apartments above. Aperitivo hour is particularly lively here: between 6 and 8 pm in summer, the piazzas fill with students and office workers and the bar counters inside and outside are packed. A few of the bars have their own distinctive spritz recipes. The atmosphere is entirely local.
