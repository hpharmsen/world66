The Orto Botanico di Padova is the oldest botanical garden in Europe still in its original location, founded in 1545 by the University as a garden for medicinal plants. The circular walled garden, surrounded by a moat, has barely changed in plan since the 16th century. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

The garden contains over 7,000 plants, including some of the oldest living specimens in Europe — several date from the 17th and 18th centuries. The original circular garden is now surrounded by more recent additions: a large greenhouse with tropical collections, a biodiversity garden, and a section of climate-change-affected species showing how the flora of the Veneto has shifted over the past century. A visit takes one to two hours. It is a short walk from the Basilica di Sant'Antonio and Prato della Valle.
