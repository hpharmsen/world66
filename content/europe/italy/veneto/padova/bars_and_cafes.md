---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

Padova's aperitivo culture is among the best in the Veneto. The city invented the Spritz — the drink of bitter liqueur, prosecco, and soda water that has since spread across the world — and the bars around the central piazzas serve it properly, with the ratio slightly different from place to place, as locals like it.

**Caffè Pedrocchi** is the historic choice — a Risorgimento-era café in a neoclassical building where the intellectual life of the city has been conducted for 200 years. Prices are higher than the student bars, but the setting is extraordinary.

**Bar Borsa** on Piazza delle Erbe is a good option for the aperitivo hour; **Enoteca al Forziere** near the Duomo has a serious wine list. The area around Via Roma and the university is full of cheap, cheerful bars serving spritz and cicchetti to students throughout the evening.
