---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Padova is on the main Venice–Milan rail line and is one of the best-connected cities in northern Italy. From Venice Santa Lucia, trains run every 15-20 minutes and the journey takes 25-30 minutes (Regionale) or 15 minutes (Frecciabianca). From Milan, high-speed trains take about 1h40. From Verona, 40 minutes.

There is no major international airport at Padova. The nearest is Venice Marco Polo Airport (VCE), about 50 km east. From the airport, take the ATVO bus to Venezia Mestre and then a train to Padova, or use the direct shuttle bus service that runs in summer.

Padova's train station (Stazione di Padova) is at the northern edge of the historic centre, about 10 minutes on foot from the Scrovegni Chapel and 20 minutes from the central piazzas.
