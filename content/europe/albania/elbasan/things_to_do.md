---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

The old walled city is the reason to stop in Elbasan. Almost everything worth seeing is inside or immediately adjacent to the [castle walls](/europe/albania/elbasan/castle_walls), so a single unhurried walk covers the ground. Start at the main western gate, walk through the [bazaar quarter](/europe/albania/elbasan/bazaar), visit the [King Mosque](/europe/albania/elbasan/king_mosque) and the [Ottoman baths](/europe/albania/elbasan/turkish_baths), and end at the [Ethnographic Museum](/europe/albania/elbasan/ethnographic_museum). Allow two to three hours to do it properly.
