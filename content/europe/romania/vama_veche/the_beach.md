The village beach stretches about a kilometre north from the old fishermen's houses, backed by low dunes and the occasional bar shack. The water is clear and calm for most of summer — the Black Sea here has no significant tides, and the coast faces east, so the morning light hits it directly. The sand is fine and pale.

The southern end of the beach, below the old part of the village, is where the unofficial clothing-optional tradition continues. The northern end tends to be more crowded, with more music and more umbrellas. Either direction from the main entrance, the beach gets noticeably quieter within ten minutes of walking.
