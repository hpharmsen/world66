---
title: "When to Go"
type: section
---

July and August are peak season and can be overwhelming — the streets are packed, finding accommodation without a reservation is difficult, and the noise at night makes sleep a lottery. Come for a specific festival and it makes sense; come for a random summer weekend and you may wonder what the fuss was about.

Late June and early September are better: warm enough to swim, less crowded, and with some of the festival atmosphere still present. Late September is genuinely lovely — the light is beautiful, the village is quiet, and you can understand what drew people here in the first place. Off-season (October to May) most places close.
