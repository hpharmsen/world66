---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Deventer rewards slow walking. The medieval centre is compact enough to cover in a morning, but the detail — the Hanseatic facades, the views from the Bergkerk towers, the carved stonework around doorways — rewards a slower pace. The city's big annual event is the Boekenmarkt in August, one of Europe's largest outdoor book markets, which transforms the streets with over 850 stalls.
