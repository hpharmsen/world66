Mount Ainslie rises 843 metres above sea level on the eastern edge of inner Canberra and provides the best overview of the city's planned geometry. The summit is reachable by a sealed road or by walking tracks from the Australian War Memorial (about 2.5 km, 45 minutes). The view from the top — looking south down the axis of the Burley Griffin plan — is essential context for understanding how the city works as a design.

Beyond the view, Mount Ainslie is part of a nature reserve within walking distance of the city centre. Walking tracks connect to Majura and Ainslie hills, forming a circuit through dry eucalypt woodland where kangaroos, echidnas, and various bird species are commonly seen. The reserve is managed by the ACT Parks and Conservation Service; access is free.
