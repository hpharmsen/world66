The Gürlevik waterfall falls about 30 metres through a narrow gorge in the mountains southeast of Erzincan, roughly 29 kilometres from the city. The surrounding valley is green and wooded in summer, making it a popular picnic destination for locals; the drive up through the mountain roads is scenic in its own right. In winter, the waterfall freezes into a column of ice that attracts ice climbers and photographers.

A small hydroelectric station was built at the foot of the falls in the 1950s, which somewhat diminishes the immediate surroundings but doesn't affect the falls themselves. The area is accessible by car via a road that branches south from the Erzincan-Kemaliye highway. In summer the weekends see local families; on weekdays you are likely to have the place largely to yourself.
