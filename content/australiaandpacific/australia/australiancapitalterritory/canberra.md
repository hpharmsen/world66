Canberra is Australia's federal capital, chosen as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne when the new federation could not agree which city deserved the prize. The city was designed from scratch by American architects Walter Burley Griffin and Marion Mahony Griffin, who won an international competition in 1912. Their plan centred on a geometric arrangement of boulevards radiating from a parliamentary axis, with [Lake Burley Griffin](/australiaandpacific/australia/australiancapitalterritory/canberra/lake_burley_griffin) as the visual heart of the city. Canberra was officially established in 1913 and the federal parliament moved here in 1927.

The result is a city unlike any other in Australia — planned rather than organic, low-rise, full of parkland, and dominated by national institutions. [Parliament House](/australiaandpacific/australia/australiancapitalterritory/canberra/parliament_house) sits atop Capital Hill, its grass roof blending into the hillside. The [Australian War Memorial](/australiaandpacific/australia/australiancapitalterritory/canberra/australian_war_memorial) closes the northern end of the city's main axis. Between them and along the lake shore stretch some of the best museums and galleries in the country: the [National Gallery of Australia](/australiaandpacific/australia/australiancapitalterritory/canberra/national_gallery_of_australia), the [National Museum of Australia](/australiaandpacific/australia/australiancapitalterritory/canberra/national_museum_of_australia), [Questacon](/australiaandpacific/australia/australiancapitalterritory/canberra/questacon), the [National Library](/australiaandpacific/australia/australiancapitalterritory/canberra/national_library_of_australia), and the [High Court](/australiaandpacific/australia/australiancapitalterritory/canberra/high_court_of_australia).

Visitors who come expecting a dull bureaucratic capital are routinely surprised. The national collections are world-class and free. The [Australian National Botanic Gardens](/australiaandpacific/australia/australiancapitalterritory/canberra/australian_national_botanic_gardens) on Black Mountain are excellent. [Mount Ainslie](/australiaandpacific/australia/australiancapitalterritory/canberra/mount_ainslie) gives a panorama of the whole city and the Burley Griffins' geometric plan laid out below. And the surrounding Australian Capital Territory contains good bushwalking in terrain that few visitors ever reach.

The city sprawls across a wide valley surrounded by hills. Central Canberra — the Parliamentary Triangle and Civic — is where tourists spend most of their time, but the planned town centres of Belconnen, Woden, and Tuggeranong are where most of the population actually lives.
