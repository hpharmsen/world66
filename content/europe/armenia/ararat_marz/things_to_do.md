---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Ararat Marz is not a city to wander at leisure — it is a province to cross purposefully, stopping at sites that reward the detour. The monastery of Khor Virap, with its dungeon pit and unobstructed view of Mount Ararat, is the obvious anchor. Around it lie the ruins of two former Armenian capitals, a wild forest reserve, and one of the finest birdwatching wetlands in the Caucasus.
