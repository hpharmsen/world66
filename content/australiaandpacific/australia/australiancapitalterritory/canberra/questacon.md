Questacon is a hands-on science and technology centre on the shores of Lake Burley Griffin, opened in 1988 as part of the bicentenary celebrations. Its 200-plus interactive exhibits cover physics, biology, mathematics, and engineering through activities that visitors operate themselves — earthquake simulators, free-fall slides, storm chambers, and demonstrations of everything from robotics to human reaction time.

The building's circular atrium gives the whole place a good flow, and the exhibits are well maintained and regularly updated. Questacon is popular with school groups and families, but the quality of the exhibits means it rewards adults too. It is free for children under five; entry fees apply for everyone else, though these are moderate by Australian attraction standards.
