Prato della Valle is one of the largest public squares in Europe — an elliptical piazza of about 90,000 square metres, with an island garden at the centre ringed by a canal and 78 statues on stone pedestals. The scale is hard to grasp from photographs. On Saturday mornings it fills with a large market; on summer evenings it becomes an outdoor living room for the city.

The square sits at the southern end of Padova's historic centre, a short walk from the Basilica di Sant'Antonio and the botanical garden. It was built on the site of an ancient Roman theatre. The statues represent Paduans of distinction — mostly from the university, the church, and the arts — though a handful of visiting dignitaries who spent time in the city are also included.
