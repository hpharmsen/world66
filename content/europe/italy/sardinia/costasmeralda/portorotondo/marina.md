The marina is the heart of Porto Rotondo — a perfectly circular natural harbour ringed by a wooden promenade lined with cafés, restaurants, and designer boutiques. In July and August the berths fill with superyachts and the promenade becomes a slow-moving catwalk. The scale is intimate: the harbour is only a few hundred metres across, and you can walk its full circumference in ten minutes.

Outside peak season the marina is significantly quieter, and the cafés on the waterfront take on a more relaxed character. The boat trips run from the marina to the beaches of the Costa Smeralda and the offshore islands — day charters are available if you want to see the coast properly.
