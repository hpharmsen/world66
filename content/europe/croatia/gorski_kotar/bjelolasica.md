Bjelolasica is Croatia's largest ski resort, sitting at around 1400 metres on the ridge south of Ogulin. It hosted the Alpine Skiing World Cup in 1986 when Zagreb bid for the 1992 Winter Olympics, and the infrastructure dates largely from that era. By international standards it is modest — a handful of lifts, groomed runs mostly suited to intermediate skiers, and reliable snow from December to March. By Adriatic-coast-in-January standards, it is remarkably accessible.

The resort village has basic accommodation, a ski school, and equipment rental. Cross-country trails extend from the main base into the forest. In summer, Bjelolasica is used as a training centre for Croatian athletes and is open for hiking, mountain biking, and zip-lining. From the top lift station on clear days you can see both the Kvarner Gulf and the distant peaks of the Velebit range.
