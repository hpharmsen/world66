---
title: "Beaches"
type: section
---

Porto Rotondo is surrounded by some of the best beaches on the Costa Smeralda. Most require a short boat trip or drive to reach, but the effort is worthwhile.

**Spiaggia di Porto Rotondo** is the closest — a sandy cove just south of the marina, calm and sheltered, with a beach bar and sun lounger rental in summer. It fills up quickly in August.

**Punta Volpe** is a larger beach about 3 km south, with shallow turquoise water and a more open aspect. The road down to it is narrow.

**Marinella** and the **Spiaggia Bianca** near Golfo Aranci are a 20-minute drive, with a longer arc of white sand and slightly fewer crowds. Golfo Aranci also has the ferry terminal for the mainland.
