The Kupa (Kolpa in Slovenian) rises from a karst spring in the forest near Razloge, close to the Slovenian border. The spring is a turquoise pool of startling colour — the water comes up from deep underground and is preternaturally clear. A short path leads through forest from the small car park to the pool, which is 3-4 metres deep in the centre.

The river quickly widens downstream and forms the international border with Slovenia for much of its length. The source itself is in a quiet, forested valley with no development beyond a picnic area and information boards. It's a small detour from the main Gorski Kotar circuit but one of those places that stays in the memory — serene, slightly otherworldly, and completely uncrowded except in high summer.
