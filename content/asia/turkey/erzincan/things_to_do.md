---
title: "Things to Do"
type: section
---

Erzincan is primarily a transit city and an outdoor base rather than a destination in itself. Its mountain setting makes it a good base for trekking in summer, skiing in winter, and whitewater rafting on the Euphrates. The main historical sights — [Altıntepe](/asia/turkey/erzincan/altintepe) and the [Mama Hatun Kümbeti](/asia/turkey/erzincan/mama_hatun_kumbet) — are worth stopping for if you're passing through. The [Gürlevik Waterfall](/asia/turkey/erzincan/girlevik_waterfall), 30km southeast, is an easy half-day excursion.
