Acton is the inner suburb north of Lake Burley Griffin that houses the Australian National University campus and the National Museum of Australia on the Acton Peninsula. The suburb has a younger character than the Parliamentary Triangle — the ANU campus brings students and researchers, and the surrounding streets have bookshops, cafes, and the general low-level bustle of a university neighbourhood.

The Acton Peninsula juts into Lake Burley Griffin and provides excellent views back across the water toward the Parliamentary Triangle. The campus itself is worth a walk — the old Melbourne Hall buildings and the newer additions create an interesting architectural mix, and the grounds include public art and open spaces that feel more organic than the city's planned ceremonial districts.
