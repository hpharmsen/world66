---
title: "Bars and Cafes"
type: section
---

The nightlife is Vama Veche's signature. Competing bars line the main street and the beach path, playing everything from Romanian folk to techno. The sound carries well into the night — if you are here to sleep early, bring earplugs or book somewhere well back from the beach. If you are here for the atmosphere, find a table at one of the open-air terrace bars around midnight and stay until it gets light.

The bar scene is informal and unpretentious by the standards of the Romanian Riviera. A beer costs a fraction of what you'd pay in Mamaia or Constanța.
