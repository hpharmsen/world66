Risnjak is the wildest part of Gorski Kotar — 64 square kilometres of old-growth beech and fir forest on a high karst plateau, with peaks above 1500 metres and a network of well-marked trails. The summit of Veliki Risnjak at 1528 m is the main hiking target: the climb takes about three hours from the park entrance at Bijela Vodica near Crni Lug, and the views over the Kvarner Gulf and on clear days to the Alps are superb.

The park is lightly visited compared to Croatian coastal parks, which means genuine quiet and a good chance of wildlife encounters — deer, bears, wild boar, and occasionally wolves are all present. The Leska educational trail (4.2 km loop) is accessible for less experienced walkers and passes through varied forest habitats. The park has basic accommodation at Bijela Vodica and is best visited May through October.
