---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Vama Veche is 7 km from the Bulgarian border, at the southern tip of Romania's Black Sea coast. The nearest large town is Mangalia, about 10 km north. From Constanța, the journey is 50 km and takes around an hour by road.

There is no direct bus service to Vama Veche from Bucharest. Most people take a bus or train to Mangalia or Constanța and then a local maxitaxi (shared minibus) south. In summer, minibuses run frequently along the coast road. Driving from Bucharest takes 3.5 to 4 hours via the A2 motorway to Constanța. Parking in the village in July and August is a serious problem — arrive early or use one of the fields outside the village that serve as informal parking lots.
