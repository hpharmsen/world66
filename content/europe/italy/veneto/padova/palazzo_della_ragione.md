The Palazzo della Ragione rises over the central piazzas of Padova, a medieval town hall raised on an arcaded ground floor that houses the daily produce market. The upper Salone is one of the largest undivided medieval halls in the world — 82 metres long, 27 metres wide, and 27 metres high — with a curved roof like an upturned ship's hull.

The hall is covered in frescoes depicting the months, the planets, and the signs of the zodiac, forming a complete medieval cosmological diagram. A large wooden horse stands near the entrance, and a stone of shame — the pietra del vituperio — marks the spot where bankrupts were traditionally humiliated. The views from the exterior staircase over the market piazzas below are excellent.
