The Lebuïnuskerk, also known as the Great Church of Deventer, is a large Gothic hall church whose origins go back to the 8th century, when St Lebuin — an Anglo-Saxon missionary — established a chapel here. The current building dates mainly from the 14th and 15th centuries and its tower, at 75 metres, is the tallest in the province of Overijssel. The church stands in its own square not far from the Brink and can be seen from across the IJssel.

The interior is broad and light, with the wide naves characteristic of the Dutch Gothic hall church style. The church contains a number of notable furnishings including a 17th-century organ. Its long history as the principal church of a prosperous Hanseatic city gives it a weight that smaller Netherlands churches lack. Guided tours run in summer and include access to the tower for views across the river plain.
