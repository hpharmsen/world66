---
title: "Getting There"
type: section
---

Porto Rotondo is on the northeastern coast of Sardinia, about 30 km north of Olbia. The nearest airport is Olbia Costa Smeralda (OLB), which has direct flights from most major Italian cities and many northern European airports, especially in summer. From the airport to Porto Rotondo is about 30 minutes by car.

There is no public transport to Porto Rotondo. You will need a car, taxi, or transfer service. From Olbia, the drive takes 25-30 minutes on the SP38. In high season, parking in the village is limited and expensive — most visitors park in the designated lots on the edge of the village and walk down.
