Khosrov Forest State Reserve covers 23,200 hectares of the Gegham, Yeranos, and Yerakh mountain ridges, rising from the Ararat plain at 700 metres to nearly 2,800 metres. The terrain shifts from semi-desert at the base through juniper and oak forests to alpine meadows at the summit. The forests themselves are Tertiary relicts — genetically ancient tree populations that survived the last ice age in the deep valleys of the reserve.

The fauna is outstanding: 192 bird species have been recorded here, along with 44 mammal species including brown bears, lynx, and wild boar. The reserve is one of the few places in the South Caucasus where the bezoar goat (wild ancestor of the domestic goat) still lives in significant numbers. Access requires a permit from the reserve administration; the Azat River gorge is the most accessible entry point and among the most scenic.
